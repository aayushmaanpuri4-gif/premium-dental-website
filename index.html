Treatments & procedures

Dental care for every stage of life

From a routine clean to restorative and orthodontic work, treatment is planned around what your teeth actually need. Select any treatment to read what it involves.

Dental Fillings

A decayed or chipped part of a tooth is cleaned out and rebuilt, bringing the tooth back to its normal shape and bite.

Root Canal Treatment

Treatment for a tooth whose inner pulp has become infected or inflamed, carried out to save the natural tooth wherever possible.

Tooth Extraction

Removal of a tooth that cannot be restored, or that is causing crowding or persistent problems, carried out with care and aftercare guidance.

Cleaning & Scaling

Professional removal of plaque and hardened tartar from the tooth surfaces and along the gum line, which brushing at home cannot reach.

Crowns & Bridges

A crown covers and protects a weakened tooth; a bridge replaces a missing tooth by anchoring to the healthy teeth beside it.

Dental Implants

A replacement tooth root placed in the jaw, which later supports a crown, bridge or denture to fill the space left by a missing tooth.

Braces & Orthodontics

Treatment to gradually move crowded, gapped or misaligned teeth into better position for both function and appearance.

Teeth Whitening

A professional treatment that lightens teeth discoloured by age, food, drink or staining, carried out under dental supervision.

Dentures

Removable replacements for missing teeth — either a full set or a partial denture that fits around the teeth you still have.

Pediatric Dentistry

Dental care for children, from first check-ups and preventive advice through to treating decay in milk teeth.

Not sure which treatment you need?

Book a consultation. Dr. Puri will examine your teeth and explain the options — including doing nothing for now, where that is the right answer.
