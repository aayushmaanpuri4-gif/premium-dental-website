Privacy Policy

Template document. This page sets out what the website itself does with your information. The sections marked below still need to be completed by the clinic to describe how patient records are handled in practice.

This policy explains how Puri Dental Clinic (“the clinic”) handles information from people who use this website. It sits alongside — and does not replace — the clinic’s obligations of confidentiality towards its patients.

Information collected through this website

The appointment form on this website asks for your name, phone number, preferred date, the treatment you are interested in, and an optional message.

This website has no server or database of its own. When you submit the form, the details you typed are assembled into a WhatsApp message addressed to the clinic, which opens in your own device for you to review and send. Nothing is transmitted anywhere until you choose to send it, and nothing is recorded by the website.

Information stored in your browser

If the clinic uses the built-in photo management tool to preview pictures on the site, those images are saved in that computer’s own browser storage only. They are never uploaded anywhere and are not visible to other visitors. No cookies are used to track visitors, and this website includes no advertising or analytics trackers.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps — the map on the location page loads only after you choose to open it. Loading it connects your browser to Google, whose own privacy policy then applies.
  • WhatsApp — used to deliver appointment requests and any message you send. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to that conversation.
  • Your phone network — if you use a call button on this site.
